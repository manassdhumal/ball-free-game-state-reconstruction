# Final Research Findings

## Research Question
Does tracking degradation harm downstream inference, and does interpolation/smoothing mitigation recover performance?

## Experimental Design
- 4 windows × 5 seeds (72–76) × 3 severities = 60 runs; each run has CLEAN, SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED, NOISE-MITIGATED conditions.

## Data and Conditions
- Canonical runs analyzed: 60 unique runspecs
- Condition observations: 180 (60 each for CLEAN, SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED, and NOISE-MITIGATED)
- Design factors: 4 windows, 5 seeds (72-76), and 3 severities (mild, moderate, severe)
- CLEAN is a robustness reference for within-run comparison, not tactical ground truth.
- SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED is generated by the benchmark degradation configuration; NOISE-MITIGATED applies the configured interpolation/smoothing pipeline.

## Main Findings
- H1: Tracking degradation harms possession inference: SUPPORTED. Observed mean possession F1 changed from 0.49199 (CLEAN) to 0.23451 (SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED), a decrease of 0.25748 across 60 paired runs.
- H2: Tracking degradation increases tactical instability: SUPPORTED. Observed tactical MAE changed from 0.00000 (CLEAN) to 0.15635 (SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED) across 60 paired runs.
- H3: Interpolation/smoothing mitigation recovers performance: SUPPORTED. Mean possession F1 improved by 0.17877 from SYNTHETIC-DEGRADED to NOISE-MITIGATED, recovering 0.69432 of the observed degradation drop. Tactical MAE also improved from 0.15635 to 0.10888, with a recovery fraction of 0.30362.

## Limitations
- Results are restricted to the specific synthetic degradation parameters and mitigation pipeline used; do not generalize beyond tested settings.
- The benchmark uses one canonical Metrica tracking source and one downstream inference implementation; external datasets and live broadcast streams were not evaluated.
- CLEAN is not a tactical ground-truth condition: tactical error is zero by construction because the degraded and mitigated outputs are compared with the clean reference generated from the same run.
- Real GSR output was not validated in this step; GSR remains BLOCKED / OPTIONAL for future work.

## What the Results Do NOT Establish
- Causality beyond the controlled experiment; transfer to other datasets or live broadcast streams.
- That mitigation restores clean-level performance in every run or under untested degradation settings.

## Conclusion
- The final aggregate analysis supports all three directional hypotheses for this benchmark: degradation reduces possession performance and increases tactical error, while mitigation recovers a meaningful but incomplete share of the loss.
